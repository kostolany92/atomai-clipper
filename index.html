How it works

Three clicks. Done.

1
📎

Clip any page

Click the extension on any article, blog post, or documentation page. AI extracts the core content automatically.

2
✂️

Pick your ideas

AI identifies key ideas and shows them as cards. Select the ones you want — skip the rest. You're always in control.

3
💎

Save to Obsidian

Each idea becomes a standalone atomic note with title, tags, backlinks, and metadata — saved directly to your vault.

Features

Built for serious note-takers

🧠

2-Stage AI Pipeline

First, AI extracts structure and key ideas. Then, it atomizes each selected idea into a standalone note. Two focused steps — better results than one-shot approaches.

🏷️

Smart Tags & Backlinks

Auto-generated tags and wikilink suggestions based on content analysis. Every note is connected, not isolated.

📦

Multiple Save Options

Save via Obsidian REST API, download as ZIP, or copy to clipboard. Works with any vault setup — local or synced.

✏️

Edit Before Saving

Review and refine every note before it hits your vault. Edit titles, body, tags, and folder — nothing is saved without your approval.
