This Privacy Policy explains how AtomAIClipper (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Chrome extension and related services (the “Service”).

If you have any questions, contact us at support@atomai-clipper.com.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and basic profile details when you sign in (e.g., via Google OAuth).
  • Usage information: clip counts and subscription status to enforce plan limits (e.g., Free plan includes 10 clips per month).
  • Technical information: basic diagnostics such as timestamps, device/browser metadata, and error logs to maintain and improve the Service.
  • Content you process: the web page content you choose to clip is processed to generate notes. We do not aim to collect more data than necessary to provide the feature.

Payment information: We do not store your full payment card details. Payments are processed by our merchant-of-record/payment provider (see “Payments”).

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the Service (clipping, note generation, saving/export features).
  • Maintain account and subscription status (Free/Pro), including enforcing monthly limits.
  • Improve reliability and security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Communicate with you about important updates, receipts, and support requests.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by LemonSqueezy (merchant of record). They may collect and process payment information and billing details according to their own policies.

When you subscribe, we receive limited information needed to grant access (e.g., purchase status, subscription status, and identifiers).

Data Sharing

We share information only as necessary to run the Service:

  • Service providers: hosting, authentication, and infrastructure providers (e.g., database/auth services) used to operate AtomAIClipper.
  • Payment provider: LemonSqueezy for payments, receipts, and subscription management.
  • Legal and safety: if required to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect users and the Service.

Data Retention

We retain account and subscription information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. We may retain certain logs for security and troubleshooting for a limited period.

Your Choices

  • Access & deletion: you can request deletion of your account data by emailing support@atomai-clipper.com.
  • Emails: you may opt out of non-essential emails. Transactional emails (e.g., receipts) may still be sent as required.

Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect information. No system is 100% secure, so please use the Service responsibly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and may provide additional notice if changes are significant.
